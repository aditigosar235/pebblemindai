Pebble Mind Journal · A simple start

Three minutes
to begin.

You do not need to be a developer to try private AI on your iPhone or iPad.

Pebble Mind lets you try supported AI models without starting with an account or API key. Choose a model, let it prepare, and ask a small question.

1. Choose a model

Open the model library and look at the size and readiness information. Choose one that suits your device and your task.

2. Let it prepare

Some models need to be downloaded first. Large AI files can use storage, memory, battery, and heat, so starting small is a good idea.

3. Ask a small question

  • “Summarize this note in three bullets.”
  • “Rewrite this message to sound warmer.”
  • “Explain this paragraph in simple language.”

Supported photos, camera captures, PDFs, and documents can also be part of the workflow. Check important information yourself because AI responses can be inaccurate.

Web search is your choice

Web search is optional. When enabled, the query is sent to the configured search service for current information and sources. You decide whether to use it.
