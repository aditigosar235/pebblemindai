Pebble Mind Journal · Privacy FAQ

Does AI need
to see everything?

Not every AI task needs to begin by sending your question to a remote service.

Pebble Mind is a free-to-start iPhone and iPad app for trying supported AI models locally. No account or API key is required for the local workflow.

A simple privacy choice

With the local workflow, your conversation is handled on your device. The app’s privacy description says chats, prompts, responses, attachments, downloaded models, and preferences are stored locally.

What if I need current information?

Web search is optional. When you choose it, your search query goes to the configured search service to retrieve current information and sources. It is a separate choice from chatting with a local model.

What can I try first?

  • Summarize a note.
  • Rewrite a message in a different tone.
  • Explain a document or paragraph.
  • Ask about a supported photo or document.
  • Brainstorm ideas or make a checklist.

Start with something small and check important answers carefully. Large models can use significant storage, memory, battery, and heat.
