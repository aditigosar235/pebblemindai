## Post — everyday privacy audience

Many people want help from AI but do not want to create an account before trying it with personal notes, questions, or documents.

Pebble Mind is a free-to-start iPhone and iPad app for chatting with supported AI models locally. No account or API key is required for the local workflow. Web search is optional, so users can choose when they want current information from the web.

The goal is simple: make private AI easier to try and easier to understand.

Download: https://apps.apple.com/us/app/pebble-mind/id6799536766

## Post — first version

We built Pebble Mind as a first step toward a calmer AI experience on iPhone and iPad. Choose a supported model, start a local conversation, and keep more control over your data. We are looking for honest feedback from people who want useful AI without unnecessary accounts or complexity.
