## Post 1 — plain language

Pebble Mind is for people who want to try AI on iPhone or iPad without starting with a hosted account. Choose a supported model, ask a small question, and keep the local workflow on your device. Web search is optional. https://apps.apple.com/us/app/pebble-mind/id6799536766

## Post 2 — technical audience

For local-AI users: Pebble Mind surfaces supported Apple and GGUF model details and readiness before download. The local workflow needs no account or API key; web search is a separate opt-in path. https://aditigosar235.github.io/pebblemindai/blog-why-we-built-private-ai.html

## Post 3 — conversation

What is the first local-AI task you would want to do on an iPhone: summarize a note, rewrite a message, explain a document, or brainstorm privately? We built Pebble Mind to make that first step less technical. Official brand account.
