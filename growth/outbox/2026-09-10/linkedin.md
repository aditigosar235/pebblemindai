# LinkedIn outbox — 2026-09-10

## Post 1 — general audience

Many people want help with notes, drafts, and questions without making a hosted AI account the first step.

Pebble Mind is a free-to-start iPhone and iPad app for trying supported AI models locally. Choose a model, check its readiness information, and start with a small task such as summarizing a note or rewriting a message.

The privacy boundary is straightforward: the local workflow runs on the device, while web search is optional and separate. We explain the choice in plain language here: https://aditigosar235.github.io/pebblemindai/blog-why-we-built-private-ai.html

Try the app: https://apps.apple.com/us/app/pebble-mind/id6799536766

## Post 2 — developer audience

Local AI should be approachable to people who are curious, not only to people who already know model tooling.

Pebble Mind brings supported Apple and GGUF models to an iPhone/iPad workflow with model details, readiness information, local model management, and additional controls in Pro. No account or API key is required for the local workflow; optional web search remains a separate path.

Product: https://apps.apple.com/us/app/pebble-mind/id6799536766
