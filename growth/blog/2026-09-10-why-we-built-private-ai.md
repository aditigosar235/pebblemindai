# Why we built a private AI app for iPhone

AI is useful, but many people do not want every question, note, or draft to begin on a remote service. We built Pebble Mind for people who want a simpler choice: try supported AI models on iPhone or iPad, without starting with an account or API key for the local workflow.

## A calmer starting point

Pebble Mind is made to feel like an app you can open and use, not an AI infrastructure project. Choose a supported model, let it prepare on your device, and begin with a small task:

- summarize a note;
- rewrite a message;
- explain a paragraph in plain language; or
- brainstorm a checklist or first draft.

The model library shows useful details before download, including size and device-fit information. You can start small and choose a model that makes sense for your device and the task in front of you.

## The local workflow

When you use a supported local model, the conversation runs on the device. Pebble Mind’s privacy description says that chats, prompts, responses, attachments, downloaded models, and preferences are stored locally. No account or API key is required to start that local workflow.

That does not mean every feature is disconnected from the internet. Web search is optional. When you turn it on, a search query goes to the configured search service so the app can retrieve current information and show sources. It is a separate, visible choice from chatting with a local model.

## Built for real everyday use

Privacy should not require technical vocabulary. You do not need to know what a model file, GGUF, or local inference means before trying Pebble Mind. Open the app, choose a model that fits, ask a small question, and decide whether the workflow is useful for you.

For people who do work with local AI already, Pebble Mind also provides a model library, readiness information, local model management, and additional controls through the one-time Pro unlock listed in the App Store. The first version focuses on making the basic workflow approachable and clear.

## What we are honest about

Local models use storage, memory, battery, and heat. Larger models may need more of each, and responses can be inaccurate or incomplete. Check important information independently, and choose a model that fits your device rather than assuming that bigger is always better.

Pebble Mind is a first version. We are keeping the promise focused: give iPhone and iPad users a straightforward way to explore supported local AI, with the privacy boundary visible and the choice of web search left to the user.
