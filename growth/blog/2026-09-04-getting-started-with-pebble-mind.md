# Getting started with Pebble Mind in three minutes

Pebble Mind is designed for people who want to try AI on iPhone or iPad without starting with an account or API key. You do not need to be a developer, and you do not need to understand how AI models are built.

## 1. Open the app and choose a model

Pebble Mind includes a model library with details to help you choose. Look at the model’s size and readiness information, then choose one that suits your device and the kind of task you want to try.

## 2. Let the model prepare

Some models need to be downloaded before you can use them. Large AI files can take storage and may use memory, battery, and heat while running. Start with a smaller supported model if you are new to local AI.

## 3. Ask a small question

Try one of these first:

- “Summarize this note in three bullets.”
- “Rewrite this message to sound warmer.”
- “Explain this paragraph in simple language.”

You can also work with supported photos, camera captures, PDFs, and documents. Results can be inaccurate, so check important information yourself.

## What about web search?

Web search is optional. If you turn it on, the query is sent to the configured search service to retrieve current information and sources. If you do not turn it on, the local workflow remains focused on the model and content on your device.

## Start free

Pebble Mind is free to start on iPhone and iPad. Pro is an optional one-time purchase for additional local-model tools.
