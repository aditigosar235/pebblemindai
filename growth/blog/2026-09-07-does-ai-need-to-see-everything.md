# Does AI need to see everything?

No—not every AI task needs to begin by sending your question to a remote service.

Pebble Mind is a free-to-start iPhone and iPad app built for people who want to try supported AI models locally. You can choose a model, let it prepare on your device, and use it for everyday tasks such as summarizing a note, rewriting a message, explaining a paragraph, or brainstorming ideas.

## A simple privacy choice

With the local workflow, your conversation is handled on your device. The app’s privacy description says that chats, prompts, responses, attachments, downloaded models, and preferences are stored locally.

You do not need an account or API key to start the local workflow.

## What if I need current information?

Web search is optional. When you choose it, your search query goes to the configured search service so the app can retrieve current information and show sources. It is a separate choice from chatting with a local model.

## What can I try first?

- Summarize a note.
- Rewrite a message in a different tone.
- Explain a document or paragraph in plain language.
- Ask about a supported photo or document.
- Brainstorm ideas or make a checklist.

Start with something small. Read important answers carefully because AI can be inaccurate or incomplete. Large models can also use significant storage, memory, battery, and heat.

Pebble Mind is a first version focused on making local AI easier to try on iPhone and iPad. If you use it, tell us what felt clear, what felt confusing, and what would make it more useful.
