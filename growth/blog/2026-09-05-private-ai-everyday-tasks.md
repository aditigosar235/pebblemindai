# Five everyday things to try with private AI on iPhone

Private AI does not have to be complicated. Pebble Mind lets you try supported AI models on iPhone and iPad, without an account or API key for the local workflow.

Here are five simple starting points.

## 1. Summarize a note

Paste in a meeting note, reading note, or long message and ask: “Summarize this in five short bullets.” You can then ask for a shorter version or a list of next steps.

## 2. Make a message warmer

Ask: “Rewrite this to sound friendly and clear.” Read the result before sending it, and keep the final wording that sounds like you.

## 3. Explain something simply

Paste a paragraph and ask: “Explain this in plain language for someone new to the topic.” This is useful for instructions, articles, and notes.

## 4. Ask about a document or image

Where supported, you can work with photos, camera captures, PDFs, and documents. Try asking for a short description or a list of the important details.

## 5. Brainstorm privately

Ask for ten ideas, a checklist, or different ways to approach a problem. Local AI can be a quiet first draft before you decide what to share.

## Keep the choice visible

Pebble Mind’s local workflow runs supported models on the device. Web search is optional. When enabled, a search query goes to the configured search service for current information and sources. Choose it when you need the web; leave it off when you do not.

Start with a small question and check important answers yourself. Pebble Mind is a first version, and your feedback helps decide what becomes easier next.
