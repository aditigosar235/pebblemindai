# Community targets and live-rule checks

Use this worksheet before each publishing cycle. Search for a current, relevant discussion first; do not treat a community as an advertising inventory. Record the exact thread URL and its local rules in the content log before posting.

| Channel | Start here | Best fit for Pebble Mind | Entry action | Hard gate |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Reddit | https://www.reddit.com/r/LocalLLaMA/ | Local-model workflow and onboarding feedback | Answer an existing question first; disclose official affiliation | Read subreddit rules and Reddit spam policy; no repetitive or unsolicited promotion |
| Reddit | https://www.reddit.com/r/privacy/ | Plain-language privacy boundary | Reply only when the question is directly relevant | Confirm self-promotion is allowed and explain optional web search accurately |
| Reddit | https://www.reddit.com/r/iOSProgramming/ | iOS developer feedback | Share a build/onboarding lesson, not a download-only ad | Read rules and disclose developer affiliation |
| Hacker News | https://news.ycombinator.com/ | Genuine first-version product story | Submit one Show HN with the privacy article and answer personally | Follow the official guidelines; do not solicit votes or use HN primarily for promotion |
| Product Hunt | https://www.producthunt.com/ | Post-launch product discussion | Use the authorized real maker account and create a draft first | Brand accounts cannot post, comment, or vote; account age/onboarding requirements apply |
| Dev.to | https://dev.to/t/localai | Developer article adaptation | Publish the useful article first, then add a brief transparent CTA | Check current community and self-promotion rules |
| Hashnode | https://hashnode.com/ | Developer article adaptation | Publish a canonical article with a practical onboarding angle | Use an authorized brand publication/profile and disclose ownership |

## Current policy references

- Reddit spam policy: https://support.reddithelp.com/hc/en-us/articles/360043504051-Spam
- Hacker News guidelines: https://news.ycombinator.com/newsguidelines.html
- Product Hunt personal vs company accounts: https://help.producthunt.com/en/articles/771527-personal-account-vs-company-account
- Product Hunt posting guide: https://help.producthunt.com/en/articles/479557-how-to-post-a-product

## Operator checklist

1. Open the target community and record its current rules URL and the target thread URL.
2. Confirm the post answers the conversation before including the App Store link.
3. Use the matching draft in `growth/outbox/`; personalize the first sentence and remove any unsupported claim.
4. Disclose official Pebble Mind ownership where relevant.
5. Publish once from the authenticated account, then record the resulting URL and platform-reported reach. If any gate is missing, mark `DRAFTED — HUMAN PUBLISH REQUIRED`.
