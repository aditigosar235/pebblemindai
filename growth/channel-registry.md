## Account rules

- Use brand display names wherever the platform permits.
- Use one genuine Pebble Mind Reddit account; never create a network of accounts.
- Product Hunt is the exception: only a real authorized person can create and operate the maker account.
- A subagent can draft and queue content but cannot claim an account is active or a post is live without a profile URL and publication evidence.
- Record every profile URL and publication URL in `../MARKETING_ACCOUNT_INVENTORY.md` and `content-log.csv`.
