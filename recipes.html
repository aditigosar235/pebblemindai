Pebble Mind turns everyday questions into focused recipes for planning, photos, reminders, wellness, contacts, music, places, and daylight. Source access is requested when you run a recipe, and supported summaries are processed on-device.

✦
Privacy is part of the workflow.

Your personal context stays on your device for supported local processing. Every proposed change is reviewable and confirmation-gated. Nearby search and optional web search are clearly disclosed network exceptions.

01 / The recipe catalog

From personal data
to useful next steps.

These are not background automations. Each recipe starts from an editable request, asks only for the source it needs, shows the evidence it found, and waits for you before a write action.

Calendar · included01

Plan what is next

Review upcoming events, locations, overlaps, and tight turnarounds. Directions open in Maps, and rescheduling stays behind your chosen time and confirmation.

Photos · included02

Find patterns in Photos

Use local visual analysis to find objects, screenshots, scenery, or likely duplicates. You select what to review; Favorites and deletion require explicit confirmation.

Apple Maps · included03

Explore nearby

Find places across a requested radius and open a result in Apple Maps. This recipe shares the search request and approximate location with Apple Maps and does not store your location.

Contacts · Pro04

Reconnect with people

Review birthdays, duplicate evidence, and saved contact channels. Pebble Mind can prepare a handoff to the app you choose, but it never sends a message or places a call for you.

Reminders · Pro05

Triage my reminders

See overdue, due-soon, later, list, priority, or undated tasks in a useful order. Completing or rescheduling a task requires reviewing and confirming the exact change.

Health · Pro · read-only06

Understand my wellness

Compare supported activity, sleep, exercise, and heart-rate readings with dated source details. Health records are never written, and this is not medical advice.

Music · Pro07

Explore my music taste

Explore saved tracks by artist, genre, release period, or request. Playlist creation appears only when requested, after you review the exact tracks and confirm.

Daylight · Pro08

Plan around daylight

Use an approximate location to estimate a conservative outdoor window around sunrise and sunset. Weather and traffic are not claimed, and no location is stored.

03 / Good to know

Private AI,
plainly explained.

Does Pebble Mind send recipe data to a cloud AI?+

Supported recipe summaries use Apple's on-device model with bounded source facts. Nearby search shares the request and approximate location with Apple Maps, and optional web search is a separate choice.

Can a recipe change my personal data?+

Only after you review the proposed target and explicitly confirm it. Health is read-only, and Pebble Mind never sends messages or places calls for you.

Is this a private AI assistant for iPhone?+

Yes. Pebble Mind is designed for iPhone and iPad, with local chat and recipe workflows that keep the main processing on-device where supported.

Which recipe features are free?+

Calendar planning, Photos analysis, and Explore nearby are included. Contacts, Reminders, Health, Music, and Daylight are Pebble Mind Pro workflows; availability can vary by platform.
